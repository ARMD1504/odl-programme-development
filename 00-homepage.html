ODL Programme Development

Welcome

Developing an ODL programme goes well beyond converting face-to-face materials into digital formats. It requires a systematic approach to planning learning outcomes, designing delivery methods, allocating Student Learning Time, developing Self-Instructional Materials, and ensuring quality assurance throughout the programme lifecycle.

This course walks you through each stage of ODL programme development — from understanding the regulatory framework to completing Table 4 and preparing your programme for accreditation. By the end, you will have developed a complete ODL programme plan for your own course.

Who Is This Course For?

This course is designed for academic staff, programme leaders, and course coordinators who are involved in planning, developing, or converting programmes for ODL delivery. Whether you are designing a new ODL programme from scratch or adapting an existing face-to-face programme for online and distance delivery, this course will guide you through every step.

No prior experience with ODL programme development is required. However, it is recommended that you complete Compulsory 1: Introduction to ODL first, as it establishes the foundational concepts that this course builds upon.

What This Course Does Not Cover

This course focuses specifically on planning and developing ODL programmes and courses. Two important areas are intentionally outside its scope because they are addressed by other courses in the series:

Introduction to ODL and its underlying pedagogy — including the theoretical foundations, history, and educational philosophy of open and distance learning — is covered in Compulsory 1: Introduction to ODL. If you are new to ODL, it is recommended that you complete that course first.

Self-Instructional Material (SIM) writing — the detailed craft of developing self-contained, self-directed learning materials for your course — is covered in Compulsory 3: Effective SIM Writing. This course references SIMs in the context of programme planning, but the practical skills of writing and structuring SIMs are developed there.

What You Will Learn

After completing this course, you will be able to:

  1. Explain the regulatory and institutional framework governing ODL programme development, including MQA COPPA requirements for programme and course classification.
  2. Distinguish between ODL delivery methods and determine how each contributes to Student Learning Time and the 80% ODL delivery threshold.
  3. Design and document an ODL course using Table 4, including the alignment of learning outcomes, delivery methods, assessment strategies, and SLT allocation.
  4. Describe the principles of effective Self-Instructional Material (SIM) development and LMS implementation for ODL courses.
  5. Outline the quality assurance, monitoring, and continuous improvement processes that sustain ODL programme quality.

Before You Begin

This course assumes that you have a working understanding of ODL concepts. If you have not yet completed Compulsory 1: Introduction to ODL, we recommend doing so first. That course covers the broader ODL landscape, theoretical foundations, and the institutional context that this course builds upon.

You will also find it helpful to have the following ready before you start:

  • A specific course in mind that you plan to develop or convert for ODL delivery
  • Access to your course's current curriculum documents, learning outcomes, and assessment plan
